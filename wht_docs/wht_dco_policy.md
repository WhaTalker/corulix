# Developer Certificate of Origin Policy

## Status

DCO sign-off is not required unless the repository's active contribution
policy explicitly enables and enforces it.

## Purpose

A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) is a per-commit attestation that a
contributor has the right to submit the contribution under the project's
contribution and licensing terms.

A typical sign-off uses:

```text
Signed-off-by: Full Name <email@example.com>
```

## Activation requirements

If WhaTalker Inc. activates DCO enforcement, the change must include:

- an explicit update to `CONTRIBUTING.md`;
- a defined merge/sign-off policy;
- automated or otherwise auditable enforcement;
- clear handling of squashed/rebased contributions;
- consistent treatment of all contributors subject to the policy.

## Relationship to provenance and CLA

DCO sign-off complements source-provenance controls but does not replace
technical review. It is also distinct from any Contributor License Agreement.
