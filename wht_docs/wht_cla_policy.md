# Contributor Agreement Policy

## Status

No Contributor License Agreement (CLA) is active for Corulix unless WhaTalker
Inc. publishes an approved agreement and an operational acceptance mechanism.

## Purpose

If a CLA is adopted, its purpose is to define the rights and representations
associated with accepted external contributions while preserving the
repository's licensing and governance model.

## Current contribution rule

The repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` is authoritative for whether external
contributions are currently accepted and what checks apply. The absence of an
active CLA must not be interpreted as an implicit alternative rights grant.

## Activation requirements

A CLA becomes active only after:

1. final terms are approved by WhaTalker Inc. and appropriate legal counsel;
2. the agreement is published in a stable public location;
3. an auditable signature/acceptance process exists;
4. repository governance enforces the requirement consistently.

## Relationship to DCO

A CLA and a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) serve different purposes. A
CLA may grant defined rights; a DCO is a contribution-origin attestation. The
applicable project contribution workflow must state explicitly which, if any,
is required.

This document is governance guidance and is not itself a contributor license
agreement.
