# ADR 0007 — GPL-3.0-only and Trademark Separation

**Status:** Superseded (see update note below)

WhaTalker-authored Corulix source was originally distributed under
`GPL-3.0-only` for the `0.1.0-alpha.1`/`0.1.0-alpha.2` source baselines,
including the `0.1.0-alpha.2` line published to crates.io. Trademark and
brand policy remained separate and did not add source-code restrictions to
the GPL license.

> Update note: superseded 2026-09-02. The current Corulix source baseline
> (version `1.0.0`) relicenses WhaTalker-authored source from
> `GPL-3.0-only` to `AGPL-3.0-only`. The published `0.1.0-alpha.2` archive
> on crates.io remains under `GPL-3.0-only` as originally released — it is
> not retroactively relicensed. See `wht_docs/wht_copyright.md` and
> `LICENSE` for the current `AGPL-3.0-only` authority, and `README.md`'s
> "Historical GPL release" section for the historical `0.1.0-alpha.2`
> record. The trademark/brand separation principle in this ADR's original
> decision is unchanged by the license migration: brand policy remains
> separate and adds no source-code restriction beyond the current
> `AGPL-3.0-only` terms.
