# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes professional behavior expectations for
WhaTalker Corulix project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and other official project channels.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and constructively;
- focus criticism on technical content rather than individuals;
- respect differences in background and experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks;
- protect private information shared in confidence;
- help maintain a productive technical environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexualized or
threatening behavior, stalking, sustained disruption, deliberate publication
of another person's private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a
professional engineering environment.

## Scope

This policy applies in official project spaces and when an individual is
representing the project in an official capacity.

## Enforcement

Suspected violations should be reported privately through the contact method
published by WhaTalker Inc. for the project. Reports are reviewed by authorized
maintainers and handled with confidentiality to the extent reasonably
possible. Responses may range from a warning to restriction from project
spaces, depending on severity and context.

This document is a project-specific conduct policy and is not a substitute for
applicable law or contractual obligations.
